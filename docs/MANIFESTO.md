# Creative Manifesto – AI Prompt Visualizer

This lab exists to explore how particles, color and motion can become visual narrative.
The goal is not optimization for its own sake, but understanding how movement can generate emotion.
Shaders are brushes. WebGL is the canvas. R3F is the instrument to tell stories.

We build under these principles:

- Show, don’t tell.
  Every idea ships as a POC and a real‑time demo.
- Explore, fail, learn.
  A broken prototype teaches more than a perfect idea in your head.
- Technology with intent.
  Nothing moves “just because”: each visual decision aims to spark curiosity.
- The user as audience.
  If someone feels something — fascination, calm, surprise — the goal is met.
- Code as craft.
  WebGL and GLSL are not only engineering; they are expressive tools.

This lab is a safe space to experiment without fear:
create, break, delete, try again — and share the process.
